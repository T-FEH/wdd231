Essential Guides

🌱 Soil Preparation

Urban Soil Challenges

City soil is often compacted, contaminated, or non-existent. Container gardening solves most issues!

Best Soil Mix Recipe

  • 60% potting soil or compost
  • 30% coconut coir or peat moss (moisture retention)
  • 10% perlite or vermiculite (drainage)

Soil Testing

Test pH levels (most vegetables prefer 6.0-7.0). Add lime to raise pH, sulfur to lower it.

Fertilization Tips

Use organic compost every 4-6 weeks. Liquid fertilizers work faster for container plants.

💧 Water Conservation

Smart Watering Techniques

  • Morning Watering: Reduces evaporation and fungal diseases
  • Deep Watering: Water thoroughly but less frequently to encourage deep roots
  • Mulching: Add 2-3 inches of mulch to retain moisture
  • Drip Irrigation: Use soaker hoses or DIY drip systems for efficiency

Rainwater Harvesting

Collect rainwater in barrels from gutters. Store up to 50 gallons for drought periods.

Self-Watering Containers

Use containers with water reservoirs or create DIY wicking systems for vacations.

🐛 Natural Pest Control

Prevent Before You Treat

  • Healthy soil = healthy plants = pest resistance
  • Proper spacing improves air circulation
  • Remove diseased leaves immediately

Organic Solutions

  • Neem Oil: Controls aphids, mites, whiteflies
  • Diatomaceous Earth: Kills slugs and crawling insects
  • Soap Spray: Mix 1 tbsp dish soap per quart water for aphids
  • Companion Planting: Marigolds repel nematodes; basil deters flies

Beneficial Insects

Attract ladybugs, lacewings, and bees with flowering herbs. They eat pests naturally!

📅 Seasonal Planting Calendar

Spring (March-May)

Plant: Lettuce, spinach, peas, radishes, carrots, herbs

Start Indoors: Tomatoes, peppers (transplant after last frost)

Summer (June-August)

Plant: Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, beans, basil, zucchini

Harvest: Spring greens before they bolt in heat

Fall (September-November)

Plant: Kale, lettuce, spinach, arugula, cilantro, garlic

Notes: Cool-season crops thrive as temperatures drop

Winter (December-February)

Indoor Options: Herbs on windowsills, microgreens, sprouts

Cold Frames: Extend growing season for hardy greens
