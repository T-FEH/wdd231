Urban Harvest Collective - Site Plan

Site Name

Urban Harvest Collective

This name represents a community-driven hub for urban dwellers to "harvest" knowledge and produce through sustainable gardening. "Urban" specifies the city-focused audience, "Harvest" ties directly to gardening outcomes, and "Collective" emphasizes the community aspect, aligning with the project's goals of building connections and self-sufficiency.

Site Purpose

The site serves as a comprehensive resource for urban dwellers interested in sustainable gardening. It provides practical guides on soil preparation, water conservation, pest control, and seasonal planting, along with community features like success stories, local garden links, and interactive tools such as weather-based planting reminders and resource maps. The goal is to empower city residents to grow their own food, reduce environmental impact, and foster community initiatives.

Scenarios

  • As a busy city professional with limited space, what are the easiest vegetables to grow on a small balcony or windowsill?
  • How can I connect with local community gardens or co-ops in my city to get started or share my experiences?

Color Scheme

  • Primary Color: #2E8B57 (Sea Green) - Used for headings, links, accent elements, and backgrounds (e.g., header, footer) to evoke growth, nature, and freshness.
  • Secondary Color: #F5F5DC (Beige) - Used for main backgrounds and subtle highlights to represent soil, earthiness, and a natural feel.
  • Neutral: #FFFFFF (White) - Used for content sections and body text backgrounds to ensure readability and contrast.

Typography

Primary Font: Roboto - A clean, modern sans-serif font used for all body text, headings, and UI elements. The 400 weight is for body text, and 700 for headings to provide hierarchy and readability.
